# Session 3923 — CI: nightly full suite, Android emulator checks, no nextest retries (#6553)

The last three checkboxes of #6553. The flaky-by-name report #6573 added is deleted: with `retries = 0` and no override, nothing in CI can pass on a retry, so it could never name a test.

## What changed

- **Nightly.** `scheduled-deep-checks.yml` has a second cron (`17 4 * * 0,2-6`) that runs only `full-suite` and `android-launch`; Monday's cron still runs every lane. `select-lanes` picks the lanes from the cron that fired and fails on a cron it does not know. The reporter judges only the nightly lanes on that cron (`file-scheduled-failures.mjs --scope`), so a weekly-only lane tracked in #3394 is neither closed nor re-reported by a run that never ran it; `scripts/file-scheduled-failures.test.mjs` pins that. The watchdog window for the workflow is now 24h/30h, re-derived from fresh lag data (its runs now start up to 8.5h late, more than the table in `check-workflow-liveness.mjs` records).
- **Which lanes.** The issue calls the nightly lane "the `--run-ignored` full suite". In the workflow `--run-ignored=only` belongs to `bench-perf-gates` (wall-clock perf gates under the `bench-slo` lane), not to `full-suite`. The issue's own dependency note ("wait until the `full-suite` lane named in #3394 is green") and "cheap, deterministic" pick `full-suite`; perf gates stay weekly. The precondition held: #3394's red `full-suite` was its prek leg on 2026-11-09, and a dispatch of that lane on `main` (run 42957014471, 2026-11-11) was green.
- **Android.** The emulator step of `android-launch-smoke.yml` (called by `release.yml` and by the deep checks) now runs `android-e2e-safe-area.mjs` and a new `android-e2e-persist.mjs` after the launch smoke: type a run-scoped marker into today's journal, `am force-stop`, relaunch, and fail unless the marker is in the app's accessibility nodes. It reads only nodes of `com.agaric.app`, so a keyboard suggestion strip echoing the marker cannot pass it. Only a dispatched run can show it works on an emulator.
- **Retries.** `profile.ci` has `retries = 0` and no override. The data: the nextest result lines of 400 `main` runs (2026-10-23 to 2026-11-12, 482 nextest jobs with a summary), 600 PR runs (from 2026-11-03, 393 jobs) and 8 deep-check `full-suite-nextest` legs. One test ever passed on a retry: `conformance_fixtures_match_backend`, 12 times, always fixture `meeting_add`'s attachment order, an ordering bug fixed by #6487; none of the 187 jobs after that fix retried anything. No timeout kill was rescued by a retry in any of them. Review deleted `scripts/nextest-flaky-report.mjs`, its test, its three job-summary steps and the JUnit uploads and `[profile.ci.junit]` that only fed it, and corrected two claims the change made false: `src-tauri/tests/AGENTS.md` ("both profiles retry") and `MainActivity.kt` ("nothing in CI runs" the safe-area script).

## Traps

- `mirror_convergence::edits_from_both_sides_converge_with_no_duplicate_and_each_ingest_undoes` failed all three tries on `main` (runs 43078442203 and 43164694573, the second after #6571) and on two PRs: a proptest finding a duplicated block after `DeleteFromFile`. Retries never rescued it, because proptest persists the failing seed and replays it on the next try, so `retries = 0` does not change it; it is a bug to fix, not a flake.
- `github.event.schedule` is the cron string exactly as written, so the nightly cron lives twice (the `on:` block cannot read `env`); `select-lanes` fails closed when they drift.
