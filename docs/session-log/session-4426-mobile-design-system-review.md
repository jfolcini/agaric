# Session 4426 — UI review on phone and desktop, and the first design-system step for mobile

**Ask.** Review every view, component and state on a phone and on a desktop, with data and from screenshots, against "modern, sleek, pleasant, intuitive". File the review as an issue and prepare the design system to work on mobile.

**Review.** Playwright on the e2e build and its mock (a Pixel 7 user agent at 412 × 915, desktop at 1440 × 900, light and dark), plus a seeded rich page, captured 348 screens. The findings are #7040: colour roles that share hues, five task-row layouts, one grey chip for every meaning, chrome stacked above content, keyboard affordances shown without a keyboard, dark popovers on cards of the same colour, and the mobile design-system gaps (M1–M10). The capture became the UI review tour (below) once the maintainer asked to rerun and extend the review at will.

**Shipped.**
- **Sheets.** Every bottom sheet stays square: it spans the screen edge to edge, and the maintainer chose square over Material's and iOS's rounded top (`docs/UX.md`). The grab handle this session first added, with a pull down to close, went again: the maintainer wants no drag handle on a phone, and the review caught a sheet pulled shut reopening shifted down. The search sheet's own handle went with it, and `usePullToDismiss` with its last user.
- **Breakpoint.** The sheet and `SheetBody` gutter switch at `md`, where `useIsMobile` stops picking sheets. They used to switch at `sm`, so from 640 to 767 px a sheet's text sat 24 px in while the view's sat at 16 px. `Card`'s section variant still drops its box at `max-sm`, matching Settings' rail, which also appears at `sm`; that is #7040 M3.
- **Pressed fill.** List rows, popover menu rows, the page menu's rows and the block menu's rows fill with `--accent` while pressed: tap highlight is off and `hover:` never fires on touch. `ContextMenuItem` was left alone: it only renders where tabs exist, never on a phone.
- **Icons.** Icon-size buttons draw a 20 px glyph on a coarse pointer. 24 icons inside icon buttons that restated the default 16 px (`h-4 w-4`) lost the class, so they follow the button; icons pinned smaller on purpose keep their size. The header's hamburger, its sync dot and the search trigger moved their negative margins so the glyph stays on the 16 px gutter.
- **Checkbox.** On a coarse pointer a task's checkbox is 18 px and starts on the text edge of a plain line; its text follows at the 16 px a desktop row keeps, and the text box, the frame while editing, starts 4 px clear of it. The 44 px target is an invisible hit area whose right edge stops at the text box, so a tap just left of the text still edits it. The first round's 44 px box (and its overhang to centre it) pushed the text to 60 px. In list rows (agenda, due panel) the checkbox hangs in the row's padding, so on a phone the title sits beside it and the chips under the title, not under the checkbox.
- **UI review tour.** `e2e/ui-review/` (a Playwright tour, its own config, the seed and the report page) and `scripts/ui-review.mjs` (`just ui-review`): every view and state, phone and desktop, light and dark. With `--base <ref>` it builds that ref in a temporary worktree, tours both builds with this checkout's tour, and writes a report that sets each changed shot's before, after and diff side by side. A shot whose setup throws is recorded as failed and the tour goes on. Neither the e2e suite nor CI runs it. The clock is fixed, so one build shoots the same pixels twice.

**Second round, from the maintainer's review of the report.**
- **Touch sizes.** A text button, toggle-group item, filter pill's ×, tag chip's × and property chip keep a compact painted shape on a coarse pointer (a step up from desktop: `Button` `default` 40, `sm` 36, `xs` 32 px tall) and take the rest of their 44 px from `touch-hit-area`, now at zero specificity so an absolute control stays absolute. Painted at 44 px, a row of them looked tall and a chip's vertical padding dwarfed its horizontal one. Icon buttons and the header bar's controls keep their 44 px boxes. The agenda's Group by and Sort by and its filter chip's × followed; on touch the filter row's wrapped line sits 16 px below the chips, clear of their hit areas.
- **Dark primary.** Softened from near-white to `oklch(0.86 0.008 70)` (11.8:1 with its label), so a filled button is no longer the brightest thing on a dark screen, as on first run.
- **Retry.** A failed list's Retry takes a new `Button` `alert` variant: label and edge in the alert's own text colour. The outline button's edge measured 1.0–1.2:1 against the red in every theme.
- **Toasts.** On a phone they sit 0.5 rem above the capture button, not 4 rem above it.
- **Journal template.** The journal header's template button is a ⋮ menu (open the template, or pick or create one). On a touch phone its rows live in the day's *Page actions* sheet, so the header gives back the row it took on its own; the mode, date and Today buttons are 44 px tall there.
- **Review tour.** An `editing` scene shoots the editing frame in block mode and page mode, on a page and on the journal day. `--notes <json>` writes a line under each changed pair; a pair without one says where on the screen it changed. A run without `--base` reshoots only this checkout against the base already shot.

**Seen, not changed:** page mode on a phone indents text 57 px inside its card (the gutter lane for the grip, chevron and checkbox), so switching modes moves the text; that predates this session.

- **CI found a data-loss race, older than this PR.** A finger's tap on the toolbar's *Block actions* opened the block menu's sheet on the lift, and the click the browser sends after the lift could land on the sheet's last row, Delete. `page-mode-touch` lost its second block 1 time in 40 to 60 under load, on this branch and on its base alike. A finger now acts on the release's click (`toolbarPressHandlers`), so that click has landed on the button before the action opens anything. The same test passed 80 times out of 80 under the same load; the unit test is shown red with the action moved back to the release. Cancelling the lift's default instead dropped that click, and with it the editor's release bookkeeping: the toolbar stayed up after the page title took the focus.
