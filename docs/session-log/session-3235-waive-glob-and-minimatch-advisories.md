# Session 3235 — waive the glob and minimatch advisories until their fixes clear the release-age gate

`main` went red in `validate / lint` because the `npm audit` hook started reporting two moderate advisories against dev-only tooling:

- **GHSA-3g3m-2fjr-6pw4 (glob 9.0.0–14.0.1).** `glob` 13 sits under `@wdio/config` and `mocha` (the e2e-tauri WDIO lane). It is fixed in `glob` 14.0.2, which keeps the API and brings `minimatch` 11.
- **GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249 (minimatch < 11.0.1).** `minimatch` 10 sits under `@stryker-mutator/core`, `mocha`, `readdir-glob` and `glob` 13. `minimatch` 3 sits under `license-checker` → `read-package-json` → `glob` 7.

**Why waivers and not an override.** Every fixed release (`glob` 14.0.0–14.0.3, `minimatch` 11.0.0–11.0.3) was published on 2026-10-27 or 28. That is inside `.npmrc`'s `min-release-age=7`, so `npm install` refuses them until about 2026-11-04. Bypassing the gate would undo the supply-chain policy it exists for.
- `minimatch` 11 also rewrites its matcher on `@isaacs/nonrecursive-glob-match` and drops the exported `AST`. Its 10.x consumers declare `^10`, so overriding them is not safe even once the gate clears.
- `license-checker`'s `minimatch` 3 has no fixed release on its major.
- `npm query 'glob:not(.dev), minimatch:not(.dev)'` is empty, so neither package is in the shipped application.

**What shipped.**
- Two `.nsprc` entries, in the `braces` entry's format. `glob` expires 2026-11-07 and names its follow-up: the override `glob@13` → `^14.0.2`. `minimatch` expires 2026-11-12, alongside `braces`.
- `osv-scanner.toml` regenerated by `node scripts/sync-audit-from-deny.mjs`.

**Traps.**
- `npm ls --omit=dev --json` walked through the worktree's symlinked `node_modules` prints a nonsense tree. Use `npm query` with `:not(.dev)` instead.
- `better-npm-audit` now says the `braces` waiver matches nothing. It stays until its own expiry, because OSV may still list the advisory.

**Suites:** `bash scripts/npm-audit-retry.sh` (exit 0), `node scripts/sync-audit-from-deny.mjs`.
