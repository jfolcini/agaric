# Session 4214 — the board scrolled under a held card mid-board; the touch drag spec raced a fling

`e2e/advanced-query-board.spec.ts` "a long press picks a card up and a drop moves it; a swipe scrolls the board" (from #6805) failed on #6809, which does not touch the board (run 43700017986): the drag overlay never appeared after the long press. It turned out to be two faults, one in the test and one in the product.

**Test race (the reported failure).** The spec's swipe flings: Chromium keeps scrolling the board on the compositor after the finger lifts. The spec then set `scrollLeft = 0`, measured the card and pressed there, but a `scrollLeft` write does not stop a fling. The failed run's trace snapshots show `scrollLeft` at 451 before the reset, then 114 … 261 while the card was measured and 325 at the press. A diagnostic run logged, for every failure, a nonzero `scrollLeft` after the reset and no card under the press point (`elementFromPoint`). Fix: the spec listens for the viewport's `scrollend`, which Chromium fires once the fling is over, before it resets and measures.

**Product bug (found once the race was gone).** With the race fixed, 1–3 in 150 repeats on 6 workers still dropped the card in the wrong column ("dropped over CANCELLED" or "over DONE"): the board had scrolled by itself during a drag between neighbouring columns, by up to its full width. dnd-kit's autoscroll (6.3.1, the latest) takes its scroll containers from the drop target (`useScrollableAncestors(overNode ?? activeNode)`, `overNode` read from a ref during render) and pairs them by index with rects that `useRects` measures in a layout effect. The card has one more scroll ancestor than a column, its column's own viewport. So once the finger is over a column, the board, index 0 of the column's ancestors, was paired with the rect at index 0 of the card's: the TODO column's viewport (256–528 on the tablet). That made the board scroll right whenever the finger was within 20% of the *column's* right edge. Logged in-page: the speeds matched dnd-kit's formula on that rect exactly (`scrollBy(1.19, 0)` at x=480), and the mismatch showed in every repeat. It usually corrected within a millisecond but sometimes lasted about 600 ms, long enough for the 5 ms interval to run the board to its end. A slow phone dragging a card to the next column hits the same window.

Fix: the board turns dnd-kit's autoscroll off and runs `useAutoScrollOnDrag`, as the block tree already does (#752), on the board's viewport along a new `x` axis and on `#main-content` along `y`. The hook reads the container's live rect each frame. It now writes a scroll position only when it has a speed, so an idle frame cannot cut short a scroll in progress. The edge zone is the hook's fixed 50 px rather than dnd-kit's 20% of the board.

Tests: the touch spec asserts the board did not scroll during the mid-board drag; a new touch spec holds a card at the board's right edge, sees the board scroll to its end and drops it on the off-screen *No value* column; a vitest case pins the `x` axis's speeds and that it never scrolls vertically.

Runs (`--trace off`): old spec 4/20 and 8/20 red at 4 workers; spec with the `scrollend` fix on the old board 1/150 and 3/150 red at 6 workers, all wrong-column drops; the new spec on the old `QueryBoard` 12/150 red at 6 workers, all on the no-scroll assertion; the fix, both touch tests ×100 at 6 workers, 200/200 green. Falsified: the edge spec reds on its scroll-to-end poll with the board's hook on `y`; the vitest case reds (300 vs 309) with the hook reading `clientY` on `x`. The board, block-dnd, journal-panels, bookmarked-pages, toolbar-and-blocks and page-mode specs passed (241). Vitest over `src/hooks`, `src/components/block-tree`, `src/editor/page-mode` and `src/components/AdvancedQuery` passed. Typecheck, oxfmt and oxlint clean.
