# Session 2807 — a heavier dark scrim, a muted disabled primary, and a bottom sheet that stops at the phone header

The maintainer said the pairing drawer looked poor on a phone in dark mode. The sheet's own content was fixed in #6010; this session took the three faults that belong to the shared chrome, after the maintainer chose the dark values.

- **Scrim.** Dialogs, alert dialogs and sheets each carried the same inline overlay string with a 50% black wash in dark themes, too light to sink a dark page's light text below the overlay's muted text. It is now one constant, `OVERLAY_SCRIM_CLASS` in `dialog-shared.ts`, at 65% in dark themes; light themes keep 20%. The palette themes (solarized-dark, dracula, one-dark-pro) get the dark value through `dark:`, since `useTheme` adds `.dark` beside their own class.
- **Disabled primary.** A light primary at half opacity on a dark ground read as a muddy grey slab. In dark themes the `default` Button variant's disabled state now takes the muted surface and muted text at full opacity, the `dark:` pattern the outline and ghost variants already use. Light themes keep half opacity, and the disabled state stays inert.
- **Tall bottom sheet.** A sheet capped at `100dvh - 2rem` slid under the phone header and cut through the view title. The bottom cap now subtracts the header's `3.5rem` and the top safe area.

`e2e/theme-overlay.spec.ts` checks the scrim and the disabled Pair button's computed colours against each theme's tokens in all six themes, plus the phone sheet's scrim in dark. Each new check was shown red by putting back the old value. Suites run: `npx vitest run src/components/ui`, the full vitest suite in eight shards, typecheck, lint, oxfmt, and the Playwright specs for phone sheets, theme overlay and icons, mobile overflow, the mobile search sheet, the new-page dialog, keyboard drawers, phone lists, views and header, the QR scanner, the bug-report dialog, pairing flows, menus and the image lightbox.
