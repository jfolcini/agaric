Here's a simple plan to get your garden ready for winter:

### 1. Clean up

1. **Remove dead plants** – they harbour pests.
2. **Rake leaves** into a compost pile.
   - Shred them first so they break down faster.
   - Avoid diseased leaves.

### 2. Protect the soil

- Add a 5–8 cm layer of mulch.
- Plant a cover crop such as winter rye.

---

**Tip:** you can check your soil with:

```bash
soil-test --ph --moisture
```
